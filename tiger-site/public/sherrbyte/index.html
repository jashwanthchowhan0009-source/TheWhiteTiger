FAQ

Straight answers.

Still unsure? Ask us anything on a short call.

What is AI Engine Optimization (AIO)?

AIO is the work of making sure AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity know your business, state your facts correctly and name you when buyers ask. We do it by publishing accurate, consistent facts on the web pages these assistants read and cite.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. AI answers are controlled by third parties and nobody can honestly guarantee them. We promise the work and the measurement, and we report your share of voice every month.

How long until we see results?

AI answers usually take 4 to 12 weeks to shift after the right facts are in place. That is why AIO Monthly has a 6-month minimum.

Do you inject data into ChatGPT?

No. Nobody can. AI assistants read the open web. We publish accurate, client-verified information through legitimate channels so AI can find it and trust it.

Is AIO different from SEO?

They overlap. Good SEO still matters for Google AI Overviews. AIO adds work SEO often skips: checking that AI crawlers can read your site, fixing wrong facts across directories and news, and measuring how often AI names you against competitors.

How much does it cost?

The audit is ₹20,000 to 35,000 one-time. AIO Monthly is ₹50,000 to 1,00,000 per month with a 6-month minimum. AI Reputation Watch is an add-on from ₹25,000 per month.
